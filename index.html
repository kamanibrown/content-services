How it works

Simple on purpose. No portals to learn, no messy threads.

1

You send footage

Drop your raw clips in Drive. Add a one-line note on what the video needs to do. That is enough to start.

2

I cut for retention

Hook up front, dead air out, captions in, sound cleaned. I edit it the way I edit my own channel.

3

You review

You get a review link. Two revision rounds are included on monthly packages. Clear notes, quick turn.

4

It goes live

You post it, or on Plus and Partner I upload and schedule it for you with title, description and thumbnail set.

Every edit includes

  • Hook in the first 2 to 3 seconds, no slow intros
  • Captions burned in and easy to read on mute
  • Tight cuts, zooms and reframing for vertical
  • Music and sound effects, licensed-safe sources
  • Clean audio, levels balanced for phone speakers
  • Export ready for YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok

Questions

Why is the test edit paid?

Because a real edit is real work, and it is the best way for both of us to know if we fit. It is $100, delivered in 48 hours, and it comes straight off month 1 if you sign within 7 days.

What do you need from me to start?

Your raw footage, your channel or brand link, and what you want the video to do. If you are on a monthly package, a simple Drive folder rhythm is all we need week to week.

Do you work outside tech?

Yes. My own channel is tech, but the editing system travels. Coaches, creators, service businesses and brands with talking-head or demo footage are a great fit. If your footage is mostly you talking and showing, I can cut it.

How fast is turnaround on monthly packages?

Edits are delivered in steady batches through the month so you always have something to post. Rush delivery is available as an add-on at +$50 per video when you need one jumped ahead.

Who owns the videos?

You do. They are your footage and your finished videos. I keep project files for revisions during our work together.
